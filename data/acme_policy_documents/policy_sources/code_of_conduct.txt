ACME CORP CODE OF CONDUCT
Policy ID: ACME-HR-003
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
The Code of Conduct establishes expected standards of professional behavior and ethical decision-making.

2. PROFESSIONAL BEHAVIOR
2.1 Employees must treat colleagues, customers, partners, and visitors respectfully.
2.2 Employees must communicate professionally and avoid abusive, threatening, or harassing behavior.
2.3 Employees are expected to follow company policies and applicable laws.

3. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST
3.1 Employees must disclose actual or potential conflicts of interest to their manager or Compliance.
3.2 Employees must not use their position for improper personal benefit.
3.3 Employees must not participate in a business decision where a personal interest could improperly influence the decision without appropriate disclosure and review.

4. GIFTS AND HOSPITALITY
4.1 Employees must not accept gifts or hospitality that could reasonably appear to influence a business decision.
4.2 Cash gifts or cash equivalents from vendors or business partners are prohibited.
4.3 Gifts that could create an obligation or conflict must be disclosed to Compliance.

5. CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION
Employees must protect confidential company, customer, employee, and partner information and use it only for authorized business purposes.

6. RECORDS AND ACCURACY
Employees must provide accurate information in company records, expense reports, time records, and other business documents.

7. FAIR DEALING
Employees must deal honestly and fairly with customers, suppliers, competitors, and colleagues and must not intentionally misrepresent material information.

8. HARASSMENT AND DISCRIMINATION
ACME Corp prohibits unlawful discrimination, harassment, and retaliation and expects employees to report concerns through available reporting channels.

9. COMPANY PROPERTY
Company property, systems, funds, and resources must be used responsibly and for authorized purposes.

10. REPORTING CONCERNS
Employees should report suspected misconduct to their manager, Human Resources, Compliance, or another designated reporting channel.

11. NON-RETALIATION
ACME Corp prohibits retaliation against employees who make good-faith reports or participate in authorized investigations.

12. VIOLATIONS
Violations may result in corrective or disciplinary action, up to and including termination, consistent with applicable policy and law.
