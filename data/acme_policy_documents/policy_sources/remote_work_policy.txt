ACME CORP REMOTE WORK POLICY
Policy ID: ACME-HR-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the rules and requirements for employees who perform work remotely from an approved alternative location.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to all ACME Corp employees who are authorized to work remotely, whether temporarily or on a recurring basis.

3. REMOTE WORK ELIGIBILITY
3.1 Employees may request remote work from an approved alternative location subject to manager approval.
3.2 Employees must have completed their initial onboarding requirements before requesting extended remote work.
3.3 Approval depends on business requirements, job responsibilities, team availability, and the employee's ability to perform duties remotely.

4. REMOTE WORK DURATION
4.1 Standard remote work from an alternative location is permitted for up to 30 consecutive calendar days per year with manager approval.
4.2 Requests exceeding 30 consecutive calendar days require written authorization from both Human Resources and the Department Head.
4.3 Employees may not divide requests into multiple periods to avoid the 30-day approval requirement.
4.4 Remote work approval does not automatically renew each year.

5. REMOTE WORK LOCATION
5.1 Employees must provide their intended remote-work location in the request.
5.2 Employees may work only from locations approved by their manager.
5.3 Employees must notify their manager before changing the approved location.
5.4 Remote work must not be performed from a location that creates unacceptable security, legal, or operational risks.

6. SECURITY AND HARDWARE REQUIREMENTS
6.1 Remote access to company systems must use company-issued hardware.
6.2 An active company-approved VPN must be used when accessing internal systems remotely.
6.3 Employees must follow all information-security and data-protection requirements.
6.4 Confidential company information must not be stored on personal devices or unauthorized cloud services.
6.5 BYOD personal laptops are prohibited for remote operations exceeding 5 business days.
6.6 Lost or stolen company equipment must be reported immediately to IT.

7. WORKING HOURS AND AVAILABILITY
7.1 Employees must maintain their normal working schedule unless an alternative schedule is approved.
7.2 Employees must remain reachable through approved company communication channels during scheduled hours.
7.3 Employees must attend required meetings.

8. REMOTE WORK APPROVAL PROCESS
8.1 Employees must submit a request before beginning remote work.
8.2 The request must include dates, location, reason, working-facility confirmation, and equipment confirmation.
8.3 Requests exceeding 30 consecutive days must also be submitted to HR and the Department Head.
8.4 Approval must be received before the requested remote-work period begins.

9. EXCEPTIONS
9.1 Exceptions may be granted when business or personal circumstances justify an exception.
9.2 Exceptions exceeding the standard 30-day duration require written approval from HR and the Department Head.
9.3 An exception for one employee does not create an entitlement for others.

10. PROHIBITED REMOTE-WORK ACTIVITIES
Employees must not use unauthorized devices, share credentials, disable required security controls, store confidential information on unauthorized devices, allow unauthorized access to company equipment, or work from an unapproved location.

11. EMPLOYEE RESPONSIBILITIES
Employees are responsible for maintaining a suitable working environment, protecting company equipment and information, following security requirements, maintaining working hours, reporting security incidents, and informing their manager of significant changes affecting remote work.

12. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Failure to comply may result in withdrawal of remote-work privileges and disciplinary action under applicable company policies.

13. POLICY QUESTIONS
Employees should contact their manager or Human Resources with questions about eligibility, approval, security, or exceptions.
