ACME CORP PERFORMANCE REVIEW POLICY
Policy ID: ACME-HR-007
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes a consistent framework for employee performance reviews and development discussions.

2. REVIEW CYCLE
2.1 Employees normally receive a formal performance review once each calendar year.
2.2 Managers may conduct additional reviews when business or development needs require them.
2.3 New employees may receive an initial review during or after their first 90 days.

3. PERFORMANCE CRITERIA
Reviews may consider job responsibilities, quality of work, achievement of objectives, collaboration, communication, reliability, and adherence to company policies.

4. GOALS
4.1 Employees and managers should establish measurable goals appropriate to the employee's role.
4.2 Goals may be updated when business priorities or responsibilities materially change.

5. REVIEW PROCESS
5.1 Employees may complete a self-assessment when requested.
5.2 Managers prepare the formal review using available performance information.
5.3 Employees should have an opportunity to discuss the review with their manager.

6. PERFORMANCE RATINGS
Where ratings are used, managers should apply the company's approved rating definitions consistently and support ratings with relevant examples.

7. PERFORMANCE IMPROVEMENT
7.1 When performance does not meet expectations, the manager may establish a documented improvement plan.
7.2 An improvement plan may identify performance gaps, expected outcomes, support, and a review period.
7.3 Completion of an improvement plan does not automatically guarantee a particular employment outcome.

8. DEVELOPMENT
Managers and employees should discuss training, skills, career interests, and development opportunities where appropriate.

9. DOCUMENTATION
Performance records must be stored in approved company systems and handled as confidential employee information.

10. FAIRNESS AND CONSISTENCY
Managers should apply review criteria consistently and avoid decisions based on protected characteristics or other prohibited factors.

11. QUESTIONS AND APPEALS
Employees who have questions about a review should first discuss them with their manager and may contact Human Resources for clarification or applicable review procedures.
