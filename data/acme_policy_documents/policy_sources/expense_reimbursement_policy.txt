ACME CORP EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Policy ID: ACME-FIN-001
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines which business expenses ACME Corp will reimburse and the approval and documentation required.

2. ELIGIBLE EXPENSES
2.1 Reasonable business travel, lodging, meals, local transportation, and approved business supplies may be reimbursable.
2.2 Expenses must have a legitimate business purpose.
2.3 Personal expenses are not reimbursable.

3. SPENDING LIMITS
3.1 Standard domestic lodging is reimbursable up to $200 per night before applicable taxes unless a higher limit is approved in advance.
3.2 Business meals are reimbursable up to $75 per person unless a higher amount is approved.
3.3 Ground transportation should use reasonable and economical options.
3.4 Expenses exceeding standard limits require documented advance approval from the employee's manager.

4. RECEIPTS
4.1 Receipts are required for individual expenses of $25 or more.
4.2 Lost receipts must be explained using the company's expense exception process.
4.3 Credit-card statements alone do not replace a required receipt unless Finance approves an exception.

5. SUBMISSION DEADLINE
5.1 Expense reports should normally be submitted within 15 calendar days after the end of the business trip or the date the expense is incurred.
5.2 Late submissions may require additional approval.

6. BUSINESS PURPOSE
Each expense must include a clear business purpose and, where applicable, the names or roles of participants.

7. PROHIBITED EXPENSES
Alcohol purchased for personal consumption, entertainment without a business purpose, traffic fines, personal upgrades, and expenses for family members are not reimbursable unless specifically approved under another company program.

8. TRAVEL ADVANCES
Travel advances require Finance approval and must be reconciled against actual documented expenses.

9. APPROVAL
Employees must submit expenses to their manager for review. Finance may reject expenses that lack documentation, exceed limits without approval, or appear unrelated to business activities.

10. FRAUD AND MISREPRESENTATION
Falsifying receipts, creating false business purposes, or submitting the same expense more than once is prohibited and may result in disciplinary action.

11. QUESTIONS
Questions about unusual expenses or exceptions should be directed to Finance before the expense is incurred whenever practical.
