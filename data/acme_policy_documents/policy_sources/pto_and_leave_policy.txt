ACME CORP PTO AND LEAVE POLICY
Policy ID: ACME-HR-002
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy establishes rules for paid time off (PTO), sick leave, leave requests, and extended leave.

2. SCOPE
This policy applies to regular ACME Corp employees. Eligibility may vary by employment classification.

3. PTO ACCRUAL
3.1 Employees accrue PTO at a rate based on employment classification and length of service.
3.2 Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of PTO per month during their first two years.
3.3 Employees with more than two years of continuous service accrue 2 days per month.
3.4 PTO accrual stops when an employee reaches a balance of 30 days.

4. PTO REQUESTS
4.1 Planned PTO should normally be requested at least 5 business days in advance.
4.2 PTO requests require manager approval before the absence begins.
4.3 Approval depends on staffing, business requirements, and previously approved leave.
4.4 Employees should not assume that submitting a request means it is approved.

5. SICK LEAVE
5.1 Eligible employees may use accrued sick leave for their own illness, medical appointments, or permitted care of an immediate family member.
5.2 Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible when sick leave is unexpected.
5.3 Employees should not disclose unnecessary private medical information to their manager.

6. EXTENDED LEAVE
6.1 Extended leave requests must be coordinated with Human Resources.
6.2 Leave protected by applicable law will be administered according to the relevant legal requirements.
6.3 Employees may be required to provide documentation where permitted by applicable law and company procedure.

7. HOLIDAYS DURING PTO
7.1 A company holiday occurring during approved PTO is not deducted as a PTO day.

8. PTO CARRYOVER
8.1 Unused PTO may carry into the following year up to a maximum balance of 30 days unless a different rule applies to the employee's classification.
8.2 Once the maximum balance is reached, additional accrual is suspended until the balance decreases.

9. CANCELLATION
9.1 Managers may request changes to approved PTO when significant business circumstances require staffing changes.
9.2 Employees should notify their manager promptly if approved leave dates change.

10. RESPONSIBILITIES
Employees are responsible for submitting accurate requests and maintaining awareness of their available balance. Managers are responsible for reviewing requests consistently and considering operational needs.

11. POLICY VIOLATIONS
Falsifying leave records or misrepresenting an absence may result in corrective or disciplinary action.

12. QUESTIONS
Questions about balances, eligibility, or extended leave should be directed to Human Resources.
