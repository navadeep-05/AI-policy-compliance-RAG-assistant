ACME CORP ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY
Policy ID: ACME-SEC-002
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines acceptable use of ACME Corp computers, networks, accounts, internet access, and communication systems.

2. AUTHORIZED USE
2.1 Company systems are primarily provided for business activities.
2.2 Limited personal use may be permitted when it does not interfere with work, consume excessive resources, create security risks, or violate company policy.

3. ACCOUNT USE
3.1 Employees must use only their own company accounts.
3.2 Account credentials must remain confidential.
3.3 Employees must not allow another person to use their account.

4. INTERNET AND EMAIL
4.1 Employees must not use company systems to send unlawful, threatening, discriminatory, or harassing material.
4.2 Employees must exercise caution when opening links or attachments.
4.3 Company email must not be used for unauthorized commercial activity.

5. SOFTWARE AND DOWNLOADS
5.1 Employees may not install unauthorized software.
5.2 Files downloaded from the internet must be obtained from trustworthy sources and scanned when required.
5.3 Pirated software and unauthorized copyrighted material must not be stored on company systems.

6. DATA AND CONTENT
6.1 Company data must be handled according to its classification.
6.2 Employees must not upload confidential company data to personal storage or unapproved external services.

7. PROHIBITED ACTIVITIES
Examples include credential sharing, unauthorized system access, deliberate malware distribution, attempts to bypass security controls, illegal activity, harassment, and excessive personal use that interferes with business operations.

8. MONITORING
ACME Corp may monitor use of company systems to the extent permitted by applicable law and company policy for security, compliance, and operational purposes.

9. EXCEPTIONS
Exceptions require approval from the appropriate system owner or IT Security.

10. VIOLATIONS
Violations may result in suspension of access, corrective action, or disciplinary action under applicable policies.
