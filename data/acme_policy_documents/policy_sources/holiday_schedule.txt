ACME CORP HOLIDAY SCHEDULE
Policy ID: ACME-HR-005
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This policy defines the standard company holidays observed by ACME Corp.

2. STANDARD HOLIDAYS
ACME Corp normally observes the following paid holidays for eligible employees:
- New Year's Day — January 1
- Memorial Day — last Monday in May
- Independence Day — July 4
- Labor Day — first Monday in September
- Thanksgiving Day — fourth Thursday in November
- Day after Thanksgiving
- Christmas Day — December 25
- One additional company-designated holiday announced annually

3. HOLIDAY OBSERVANCE
3.1 If a holiday falls on a weekend, ACME Corp will announce the observed weekday when applicable.
3.2 The annual holiday calendar published by Human Resources is the controlling schedule for the applicable year.

4. ELIGIBILITY
4.1 Holiday eligibility depends on employment classification and scheduled work status.
4.2 Employees on approved leave should consult HR regarding how the holiday affects their leave balance.

5. WORKING ON A HOLIDAY
5.1 Certain business functions may require employees to work on a company holiday.
5.2 Compensation or alternative time off for holiday work depends on employment classification and applicable company rules.

6. HOLIDAY AND PTO
A company holiday that occurs during approved PTO is normally not deducted from the employee's PTO balance.

7. PART-TIME EMPLOYEES
Holiday eligibility and pay for part-time employees are determined according to applicable employment terms and law.

8. SCHEDULE CHANGES
Human Resources may publish changes or additions to the annual holiday calendar when business or operational requirements require them.

9. QUESTIONS
Employees should contact Human Resources for questions about holiday eligibility or the current annual calendar.
