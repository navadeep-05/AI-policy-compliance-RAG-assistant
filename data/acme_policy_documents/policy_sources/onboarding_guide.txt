ACME CORP EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING GUIDE
Policy ID: ACME-HR-006
Version: 2.0
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

1. PURPOSE
This guide defines the standard onboarding activities expected during an employee's first weeks at ACME Corp.

2. BEFORE THE START DATE
2.1 New employees must complete required employment documentation.
2.2 Required identity, payroll, and contact information must be submitted through approved channels.
2.3 IT may prepare approved equipment and account access before the start date.

3. FIRST DAY
3.1 Employees must complete required orientation activities.
3.2 Employees must activate their company account and configure required authentication.
3.3 Employees must review applicable security, acceptable-use, and conduct policies.

4. FIRST WEEK
4.1 Employees should complete mandatory training assigned by Human Resources or their department.
4.2 Employees should meet their manager and understand role responsibilities and working arrangements.
4.3 Employees must verify that required systems and tools are available.

5. SECURITY TRAINING
Employees must complete assigned information-security and privacy training within the deadline specified by the training system.

6. EQUIPMENT
6.1 Company-issued equipment must be used according to security and acceptable-use policies.
6.2 Employees must not install unauthorized software on company-managed devices.
6.3 Equipment problems should be reported to IT through the approved support process.

7. ACCESS REQUESTS
7.1 Access to systems is granted according to job responsibilities.
7.2 Employees must not request or use access that is unnecessary for their role.
7.3 Access changes should be requested through the manager and approved access process.

8. PROBATION / INITIAL REVIEW
Managers may conduct an initial performance or onboarding review during the first 90 days.

9. COMPLETION
Onboarding is considered complete when required documentation, training, system access, and role-specific orientation activities have been completed.

10. QUESTIONS
Employees should contact their manager, Human Resources, or IT depending on the issue.
